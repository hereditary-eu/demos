In [HEREDITARY](https://hereditary-project.eu/), we build various prototypes and demos to showcase visualisation possibilities.
Many of the demos are developed as part of the Work Package 5 (WP5) efforts, which focusses on visualisation.

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Demo
    title="Clusters in Focus"
    image={ClustersInFocusImage}
    link="https://hereditary.cgv.tugraz.at/cif"
    code="https://github.com/hereditary-eu/ClustersInFocus"
    paper="https://doi.org/10.2312/vcbm.20251250"
    paper-title="Clusters in Focus: A Simple and Robust Detail-On-Demand Dashboard for Patient Data"
    contact="Peter Waldert"
  >
    Exploring tabular datasets to understand how different feature pairs
    partition data into meaningful cohorts is crucial in domains such as
    biomarker discovery, yet comparing clusters across multiple feature pair
    projections is challenging. We introduce Clusters in Focus, an interactive
    visual analytics dashboard designed to address this gap.
  </Demo>
  <Demo
    title="Neurodegen-Vis"
    image={NeurodegenVisImage}
    link="https://hereditary.cgv.tugraz.at/neurodegen-vis/"
    code="https://github.com/hereditary-eu/Neurodegen-Vis"
    paper="https://tugraz.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/0c242154-71e2-4b01-abd8-6f386da18f4e"
    paper-title="Privacy-Preserving Visual Exploration of Healthcare Data with a Language Model Integration"
    contact="Peter Waldert"
  >
    Healthcare records, measurements and datasets can quickly become highly
    complex in terms of storage, anonymisation and interpretation. With
    Neurodegen-Vis, we present a practical toolkit to anonymise, visualise and
    help interpret medical data in a Visual Analytics dashboard, with seamless
    integration of a Large Language Model (LLM).
  </Demo>
  <Demo
    title="OnSET"
    image={OnSetImage}
    link="http://onset.cgv.tugraz.at/"
    code="https://github.com/hereditary-eu/onset"
    paper="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3726302.3730148"
    paper-title="OnSET: Ontology and Semantic Exploration Toolkit"
    contact="Benedikt Kantz"
  >
    OnSET is designed to enable a more explorative ontology querying experience
    for non-expert users, and to build a quick understanding of the scale and
    nature of a KG that is in use. The toolkit consists of multiple smaller
    systems, complimenting each other.
  </Demo>
  <Demo
    title="ParamInter"
    image={ParamInterImage}
    link="https://hereditary.cgv.tugraz.at/param-inter/"
    code="https://github.com/Dakantz/ParamInter"
    paper="https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19202"
    paper-title="AlloyInter: Visualising Alloy Mixture Interpolations in t-SNE Representations"
    contact="Benedikt Kantz"
  >
    ParamInter is a novel toolset to explore continous input-output parameter
    sets interactively through guidance by XAI and UCQ. The approach
    interpolates between selected input samples over an embedded subspace,
    guiding the user towards required solutions for their target output
    parameters.
  </Demo>
  <Demo
    title="Gut-Brain Viewer"
    image={GutBrainImage}
    link="https://hereditary.cgv.tugraz.at/gut-brain-viewer"
    code="https://github.com/hereditary-eu/gut-brain-visualization"
    contact="Casper van Leeuwen"
  >
    The Gut-Brain Viewer visualizes gut microbiota-brain links from LICA outputs
    via three linked views. An interactive microbiota matrix shows organism
    Z-scores per component with thresholding and selection. A modality
    stacked-bar view displays percent contributions and loads the selected
    modality. Interactive brain slices and volumetric renders show voxel
    Z-scores for SN, ECN, or DMN from 4D NIfTI files.
  </Demo>
  <Demo
    title="Multi-Component State Model Visualisation"
    image={InSilicoImage}
    link="https://in-silico-cancer-cell.waldert.at/"
    code="https://github.com/MrP01/InSilicoCancerCell/"
    paper="https://conferences.eg.org/vcbm2025/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2025/09/Optimization-of-a-Parametrized-Time-Dependent-Hidden-Markov-Model-by-Example-of-an-Electrophysiological-Cancer-Cell.pdf"
    paper-title="Optimization of a Parametrized, Time-Dependent Hidden Markov Model by Example of an Electrophysiological Cancer Cell"
    contact="Peter Waldert"
  >
    This component is concerned with the visualisation of a multi-component
    state model coupled (biological) system. Each component can be modelled
    through its own individual state model, including dependencies on the
    environment such as ion concentrations, voltages or other variables.
  </Demo>
  <Demo
    title="Differential Cell Signaling"
    image={DiffCellSigImage}
    contact="Gaia Tussardi"
  >
    In medical applications, the increasing availability of single-cell RNA
    sequencing data from pathological and healthy samples has led to the
    development of methods for differential analyses, i.e. extensions of classic
    bioinformatics analyses which identify significant biological alterations in
    the pathological condition with respect to an healthy reference. A first
    draft of the dashboard shows a preview of the graph.
  </Demo>
  <Demo title="Alvis" image={AlvisImage} contact="Peter Waldert">
    Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS) is a fatal neurodegenerative disease
    affecting over 300,000 people worldwide. Currently, there is no cure—only
    treatments aimed at improving patients' quality of life. We introduce ALviS,
    a prototype designed for analyzing ALS datasets, providing a foundational
    framework for visualizing and exploring the multivariate ALS dataset.
  </Demo>
  <Demo
    title="Neuronal Brain Simulation"
    image={NeuronalImage}
    contact="Peter Waldert"
  >
    The Brain Connectome and Activity Data dashboard visualises a simulation of
    the brain. On the left, we have a shared view between the neuron graph and a
    perspective view. On the right, the varying quantities described above are
    displayed, for one selected neuron, as a coloured line chart. The colour
    intensity is used to indicate areas of high activity, although that can be
    adjusted.
  </Demo>
  <Demo
    title="Talk to Your Graph"
    image={TTYGImage}
    link="https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/talk-to-graph.html"
    contact="Ivelina Nikolova-Koleva"
  >
    Natural language querying (NLQ) and visual querying (VQ) are related as
    user-friendly paradigms for interacting with a KG, each mapping intuitive
    user input (language or visuals) to the same semantic graph model. The
    proposed approach Talk To Your Graph (TTYG) overcomes the need for SPARQL
    query writing and any knowledge about the technologies and particular
    semantic model and ontologies.
  </Demo>
