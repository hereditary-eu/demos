Synthetic data generation is the process of generating a new synthetic
dataset based on the statistical properties of a confidential existing
dataset. Differential privacy is the property of such mechanism that
establishes the level of privacy, when sharing such data. To ensure
differential privacy, noise is injected into the statistics learned from
the dataset, entailing a trade-off between privacy and utility. Privacy is
then measured via a set of privacy metrics that usually establish a lower
bound on a few aspects of the privacy-utility tradeoff. PrivEval is a tool
to make privacy assessment more transparent through evaluation of several
privacy metrics on both a single user and the overall dataset.
