Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS) is a fatal neurodegenerative disease
affecting over 300,000 people worldwide. Currently, there is no cure—only
treatments aimed at improving patients' quality of life. We introduce
ALviS, a prototype designed for analyzing ALS datasets, providing a
foundational framework for visualizing and exploring the multivariate ALS
dataset.
