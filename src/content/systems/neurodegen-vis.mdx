Healthcare records, measurements and datasets can quickly become highly
complex in terms of storage, anonymisation and interpretation. With
Neurodegen-Vis, we present a practical toolkit to anonymise, visualise and
help interpret medical data in a Visual Analytics dashboard, with seamless
integration of a Large Language Model (LLM).
