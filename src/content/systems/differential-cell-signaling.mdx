An interactive dashboard for exploring differential cell-signaling
networks by integrating cell-cell communication inference with
transcription factor (TF) activities inferred from scRNA-seq. It
highlights the ligands, receptors, TFs, and cell types that drive disease
mechanisms, traces signaling loops, and supports on-the-fly GO, KEGG, and
Reactome over-representation analyses. An Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis
(ALS) case study is included, enabling the biomedical inspection of
sporadic ALS, familial ALS, and their contrasts.
