The Gut-Brain Viewer visualizes gut microbiota-brain links from LICA
outputs via three linked views. An interactive microbiota matrix shows
organism Z-scores per component with thresholding and selection. A
modality stacked-bar view displays percent contributions and loads the
selected modality. Interactive brain slices and volumetric renders show
voxel Z-scores for SN, ECN, or DMN from 4D NIfTI files.
