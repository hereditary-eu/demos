The Neuronal Lens tool visualises a simulation of the brain, focussing on
the brain connectome and activity data. On the left, we have a shared view
between the neuron graph and a perspective view, with an integrated lens.
On the right, the varying quantities described above are displayed, for
one selected neuron, as a coloured line chart. The colour intensity is
used to indicate areas of high activity, although that can be adjusted.
